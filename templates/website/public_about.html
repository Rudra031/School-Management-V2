Child Safety & Care

Values & Safety Standards

Our strict protocols ensuring every child is safe, respected, and nurtured.

100% CCTV & Child Protection Protocol

Full CCTV coverage across classrooms, activity hubs, and playparks. Female attendants in all washrooms and background-verified staff.

Zero Tolerance for Harsh Discipline

No corporal punishment or shaming. Emotional behavioral guidance through positive reinforcement, empathy circles, and child psychology support.

Organic Junior Nutrition & Hygiene

Nut-free, sanitized cafeteria providing fresh fruits and wholesome warm meals curated by pediatric dieticians.

24/7 Pediatric Infirmary & Doctor-on-Call

Full-time certified pediatric nurses, child emergency care beds, regular dental and eyesight health checkup camps.
