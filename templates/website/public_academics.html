EARLY EXPLORERS (PLAYGROUP & NURSERY) Ages 2.5 – 3.5 Years

Sensory Wonder & Social Care

Gentle transition from home to school. We nurture social confidence, fine motor skills, and sensory exploration through play dough, water tables, rhymes, and loving teacher hugs.

CORE ACTIVITIES
  • Tactile sensory sandpits, water basins, and clay sculpting
  • Musical rhythm circles, puppet storytelling, and speech development
  • Independence skills: handwashing, tidy-up habits, and potty confidence

KINDERGARTEN (LKG & UKG) Ages 4 – 5 Years

Phonics & Early Number Sense

Structured Jolly Phonics immersion to turn young learners into fluent early readers. Visual mathematics with Singapore math blocks builds intuitive calculation and logic.

CORE ACTIVITIES
  • 42 Jolly Phonics sounds, blending, sight words, and reading clubs
  • Number sense, counting beads, patterns, and shape geometry
  • Junior nature discovery: seed sprouting, weather charts, and mini experiments

LOWER PRIMARY (GRADES 1 – 3) Ages 6 – 8 Years

Fluent Literacy & STEM Discovery

Transition to formal subjects including English, Hindi, Mathematics, and Environmental Studies (EVS). Introduces block-based logic, visual arts, and weekly swimming lessons.

CORE ACTIVITIES
  • Reading comprehension, creative journaling, and vocabulary enrichment
  • Hands-on STEM lab sandbox and Scratch Jr. coding puzzles
  • EVS nature walks, botanical garden studies, and recycling projects

UPPER PRIMARY (GRADES 4 – 5) Ages 9 – 10 Years

Inquiry, Logic & Leadership

Prepares young scholars for middle school transition through project-based inquiries, inter-house debating, junior robotics competitions, and second language mastery.

CORE ACTIVITIES
  • Lego Mindstorms Robotics & Junior AI logic blocks
